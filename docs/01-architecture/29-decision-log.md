## Open questions for the owner
1. Companies expected in year 1 and year 3; size of the largest (users, invoices per day).
2. How many companies do you expect per cell before you want a second cluster, and which customers might need a dedicated cell (D-043)?
3. Do any clients need physical separation (their own cluster, key or region)?
4. Will one owner run several companies under one login with combined reports?
5. Cloud and India region (D-014); which GSP vendor (D-013).
6. Team size and skills (Express, Flutter, MongoDB).
7. Hide customer and employee names at field level too (Atlas supports Queryable Encryption; limits search)?
8. Confirm D-008 (24-hour stale permissions), D-009 (device-only drafts), D-022 (which roles a manager may create) and D-023 (super admin sees company data only through a support grant).
